description: >-
  This skill should be used when an AI agent, skill, or multi-agent workflow is being deployed,
  connected to external benchmarks or third-party systems, or put through evaluation, and a human
  governor needs a centralized control tower to make AI behavior observable, auditable, and
  accountable. It acts as the "人治 AI 世界的治理仪表盘（control tower）": it orchestrates
  existing guardrail skills (external-benchmark-safety, skills-security-check,
  verified-agent-identity, authz-code-design) and ties behavioral auditability to the
  ai-consciousness + ai-grader self-assessment loop — making AI self-audit an *instrument of human
  governance*, not a replacement for it. Trigger proactively for regulated or international-business
  contexts (e.g., medical-device international business needing EU AI Act / FDA traceability and
  human-oversight compliance).
